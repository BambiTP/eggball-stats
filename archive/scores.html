Eggball final scores: TPM vs playeggball

Short answer: playeggball ranked games end with about 1.6 more goals (21.2 vs 19.6) and are more one-sided (the winner is ahead by 6.0 on average vs 4.9). Goals come at the same speed on both (2.68 vs 2.64 per minute); the extra goals mostly come from playeggball games running longer.

Read this first

  • The two sets are from different times. TPM games: May 2021 to Apr 2025. playeggball games: Oct 19 to Nov 02, 2026. Players, skill and habits may have changed in between.
  • playeggball has only 548 ranked games so far against 14,420 TPM games, so its numbers are less certain (but still enough for the gaps below to be real, see the end).
  • The rules are not identical. TPM games stop at 8:00 and can end tied. playeggball games never tie and can run past 8 minutes.
  • Scores include Raptor Boats (2-point goals) on both sides; they are not split out here.

Is the difference real or just luck?

Yes, it is real. With this many games, the gap in total goals is 6.5 times bigger than random variation would explain, and the gap in margin is 7.1 times bigger (anything over about 2 is usually counted as real). That says the averages really differ, not why: the caveats above (different years, different rules) could be part of the reason.

How this was made

  • TPM games: every match in a full copy of tagpro.eu (all years) on the eggball maps ("eggball" and the older "Egg Ball") where a team name contains "tpm". Found 14,546; 126 never finished and are left out. Team pairings:
    • EGGNA vs tpm.gg: 11,983 games
    • Blue vs TPM: 1,732 games
    • EGGOCE vs tpm.gg: 801 games
    • EGGEU vs tpm.gg: 30 games
  • playeggball games: every ranked game collected from playeggball.com since we started saving them (checked hourly). Casual games are left out.
  • Scores are the final team scores each site records.
